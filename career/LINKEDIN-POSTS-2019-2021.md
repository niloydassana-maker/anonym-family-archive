## 2020-09-26

Motherless at 4, marriage at 14, mother at 23, widow at 25, defender of Jhansi at 29, battlefield warrior against British forces at 30, fought battles while tying her son to her back, martyrdom at 30! That is the brave Rani Lakshmi Bai of Jhansi who was an inspiration for all freedom fighters. She continues to inspire us and will continue to inspire future generations till eternity.
