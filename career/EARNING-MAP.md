## 4. LOAN TRIAGE HONESTY (₹50,00,000)

### 4a. The arithmetic that governs everything

At ~10.5% interest, 50L costs **~₹43,750/month in interest alone.** Full EMI on a 15-year schedule ≈ ₹55k/month. Therefore: **no income below ₹1L/month is credible to a bank, and income restart must come BEFORE the restructuring negotiation bears fruit.** Sequence is law: fast money → income proof → bank talk → restructure. (The one exception: the hardship letter below goes out TONIGHT — starting the paper trail costs nothing and stops the "ignoring the bank" failure mode.)

### 4b. What happens if nothing is done (so it's said once, plainly)

Term-loan EMIs unpaid >90 consecutive days → account classified **NPA** → recovery notices, possible action on any collateral, CIBIL wrecked for 7+ years, and restructuring becomes a settlement negotiation instead of a restructure. Every day of silence makes the bank's model of you worse. Answering calls is not weakness; it's the cheapest move on the board.

### 4c. The India-specific playbook

1. **Inventory the 50L tonight (30 minutes):** one page — lender, product (secured home/property loan vs unsecured personal loans vs credit cards), outstanding, rate, EMI, overdue days for each. Everything downstream depends on secured vs unsecured. Secured (home/property) = highest priority; that's an asset at risk. Unsecured = bank's problem too; they have more incentive to settle.
2. **Don't ignore calls; control the channel.** Take calls politely, note date/time/agent name, and move everything to email: "Please send the demand and options to my email; I will respond in writing." RBI rules for recovery agents: calls only 8 AM–7 PM, no harassment/abuse, no threats. Violations → complain to the bank's nodal officer in writing, then RBI Integrated Ombudsman (cms.rbi.org.in). Never pay cash to any agent — only to the bank account, with receipt.
3. **Hardship letter in writing TONIGHT (template below).** Send to the bank's official email + nodal officer. Ask explicitly for: (a) restructuring under the bank's board-approved policy (RBI's Prudential Framework for Resolution of Stressed Assets lets lenders restructure before default/NPA — banks implement via their own policies), (b) EMI reduction via tenor extension, (c) a 3–6 month interest-only moratorium, (d) rate reset. Ask what documentation they need — and what you qualify for **while the account is still standard**, because after NPA the menu shrinks to settlements.
4. **Prove income restart, then negotiate from strength.** Even ₹30k–50k/month of documented income (workshop receipts, contract invoices, Topmate payouts) changes the conversation from "defaulter" to "temporary stress, recovering." Pay even one part-EMI with that income and reference it in writing: "demonstrating good faith while restructuring is assessed."
5. **One-Time Settlement (OTS) — know what it is before you want it:** banks settle unsecured loans at ~90–100% of outstanding typically, sometimes less for deep-NPA accounts. CIBIL shows "Settled" — worse than "Closed," blocks fresh credit for years. OTS is the emergency exit, not the plan. Restructure ("Restructured" tag, also visible ~7 years) preserves more standing than OTS.
6. **Consolidation — only if income proof exists:** a balance transfer of expensive unsecured loans (credit cards 36–42% → personal loan 11–14%) saves real money once salary/income proof returns. **Warning:** consolidating unsecured debt into a loan secured against home/property converts unsecured (settled via OTS if it comes to that) into secured (asset at risk). Do not cross that line without income that covers the new EMI.
7. **Absolute rules:** no new loan to pay old EMIs (unsecured or otherwise), no loan apps ever, no withdrawal from whatever remains of savings before the secured-loan decision is made, everything in writing, every call logged.

**Template — Hardship/Restructuring letter (send tonight, fill the brackets):**

I am actively restarting income (consulting engagements now underway; proof available within [30] days). I am committed to full repayment and request that the account not be downgraded while this request is assessed. Kindly respond in writing to this email. [Name, phone, address, account no.]
