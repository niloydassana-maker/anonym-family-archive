## WHY NOT `crush run`

Measured today, all three:
1. crush's system prompt is **111,464 tokens** — Groq free answered **HTTP 413**
   (`Limit 8000 TPM, Requested 111464`). No free endpoint can accept it.
2. NVIDIA reasoning models (nemotron-super-120b, deepseek-v4.1-flash) answer with
   crush's **context-summary schema** ("## Current State / ## Technical Context")
   instead of emitting tool calls; deepseek then stalled at **0.0% CPU for 9 min**.
3. Non-reasoning models (llama-3.2-11b) **fabricate** tool executions and leak
   system reminders.

`crush run` itself is fine — it spawned tools correctly (`python _now.py` at 22:11:59).
The **model + the 111k prompt** are the wall.

**Do not run a fourth driver test. Do not try another model.**
